# Lab 7.1: Enterprise AI Governance, EU AI Act & Shadow AI Policy Framework
*The Ethical Hacker's Playbook — Chapter 7 (2026 Edition)*

This lab provides enterprise-ready governance templates for security teams deploying and auditing AI tooling.

---

## 📋 Included Governance Assets

1. **`enterprise_ai_security_policy.md`**:
   - Comprehensive corporate policy governing Generative AI usage, prompt hygiene, data classification, and shadow AI prohibition.
   - **EU AI Act (High-Risk Classification)** compliance checklist for automated security tooling (Article 9, 14, 15).
   - **NIST AI 600-1** risk mitigation mapping.
   - Model Context Protocol (MCP) and third-party API tool authorization policy.

2. **`rules_of_engagement_ai_redteam.md`**:
   - Legal authorization and scope template for conducting authorized adversarial red teaming against AI systems and autonomous agents.

---

## 🎯 How to Use in Your Organization
- Customize the bracketed parameters (`[COMPANY_NAME]`, `[CISO_CONTACT]`) in `enterprise_ai_security_policy.md`.
- Present to your Information Security Steering Committee and Legal/Compliance teams.
